Frequently asked questions

Is this AI poster generator free?

Yes. The generator is completely free and does not require an account. Open the page, type your idea, and download your poster.

How long does it take to generate a poster?

Most posters are ready in 5 to 20 seconds. If the AI image service is busy, the generator instantly falls back to a smart gradient background so you always get a result.

Can I use the posters I create commercially?

The posters you create are yours to use for social media, events and promotions. If you plan large-scale commercial use, please review the AI-imagery rules that apply in your country or industry.

What poster sizes are supported?

Four sizes: Poster 4:5 (1080×1350) for Instagram, Square 1:1 (1080×1080), Story 9:16 (1080×1920) for Reels and TikTok, and Wide 16:9 (1920×1080) for screens and banners.

Will the AI write the text on my poster?

The AI paints the background artwork. Your title, subtitle and call-to-action are typeset on top automatically, which keeps every word sharp and correctly spelled — a common weakness of pure image generators.
